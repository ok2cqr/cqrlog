## Done
- **Stage 5, commit 85f33f6.** Club membership of the heard station after its call
  (`OK1ABC (EPC, SOTA)`) in all graphical band maps and in the text band map.

  8 new unit tests (`tLogCache`, `tCheckQueue`). Petr tested: works.
- **Stage 6, uncommitted.** Grayline popup got `RBN source for "Connect to RBN"`

  `h21.html#bh19g`.

## Decisions
- Membership reuses `RbnLogCache` instead of a new cache unit: one lock, one
  thread, one invalidation path; per-club invalidation is "all membership
  entries", imports are rare.
- The `frmBandMap` dependencies of the producers stay: the text map is a
  singleton by design (its thread uses the global ~60 times).
- The Preferences fields `[RBN] Server/login` stay as the fallback source of
  the Grayline; the RBN tab layout is Petr's WIP (lint reports its anchors),
  so no controls were added there.
